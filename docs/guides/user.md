# User guide

## Request and activate an account

1. Open the sign-in page and choose **Create account**.
2. Enter your first name, last name, email address, and a note answering **Which system should the admin assign you to?**
3. Submit the request. An identity administrator reviews it and can assign access to a system.
4. After approval, open the setup email within 48 hours. The link verifies your email address and lets you choose a password of at least 15 characters.

If your request is still pending, contact the administrator who handles access to your project. If an approved setup link expires, **Forgot password?** can send a new setup link.

## Sign in and sign out

Start from the project you want to use. It sends you to the issuer to sign in, then returns you to the project. You can also open the issuer directly to see your account. Your access and role can differ by project.

Use **Sign out** on the issuer to end its sign-in session. Use the project's own sign-out control to end your session there. If a project denies access, ask your identity administrator to check your account, service grant and role.

## Change or recover a password

Open **Forgot password?** and enter the email tied to your account. If the account is eligible, you will receive a single-use link valid for 45 minutes. Use the link to choose and confirm your new password, then sign in again. The change closes your existing issuer sessions and invalidates outstanding recovery links.

If the link fails, request a new one. A browser with JavaScript disabled can use the code provided in the email on the password reset page. If the email is missing, check the junk folder and contact your administrator using the usual channel.
